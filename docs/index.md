
# Knowledge Hub

A practical, open knowledge base exploring software engineering, technical program management, and geospatial technology.

## Explore

- **Technical Program Management:** Frameworks for technical delivery, risk management, architecture reviews, and cross-team execution.
- **Software Engineering:** Hands-on notes and concepts from courses, implementations, and experiments.
- **GIS & Remote Sensing:** Reproducible Earth observation projects and geospatial analysis.
- **Projects:** Practical implementations, findings, and lessons learned.

## How this knowledge hub works

The goal is to explain technical concepts clearly, demonstrate them through practical examples, and connect them to real-world engineering and program delivery.

Content is developed incrementally. Where possible, examples use open-source tools and publicly accessible resources.
